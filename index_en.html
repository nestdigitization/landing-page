Frequently Asked Questions

What types of museums can use NEST?

+

NEST is designed to adapt to any type of museum. We started with natural history museums as early adopters, but our platform is fully customizable for art, archaeology, ethnography, and any other type of cultural heritage collection.

Is technical training required to use the software?

+

No, our software is designed to be intuitive and user-friendly. We offer brief initial training and detailed support manuals. Museum staff can start digitizing after just a few hours of training.

How does artificial intelligence work in the system?

+

AI acts as an assistant: it automatically recognizes objects and species, reads labels via OCR, and suggests data standardization. However, all final decisions remain under human operator control, ensuring scientific accuracy and content quality.

What cataloging standards do you support?

+

NEST supports all major internationally recognized museum and scientific standards (e.g. Darwin Core Darwin Core (DwC) International standard for sharing biodiversity data. For Art Museums: Descriptive standards such as LIDO or CDWA. ). The platform can be configured to comply with your museum's specific standards or national guidelines.

When will the full version be available?

+

We currently have a working prototype and are preparing for the beta launch. By subscribing to the newsletter you will receive timely updates on the launch and gain priority access to the platform.

Can I see a demo of the system?

+

Absolutely! Once you sign up for beta access via the newsletter, you can request a personalized demo. We will show you how NEST can be specifically tailored to your museum's needs.
